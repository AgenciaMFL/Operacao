How much does a new pool cost?

Every pool is different — the price depends on size, shape, finishes, features and your site. After a free on-site visit, we give you a transparent, itemized quote so you know exactly what you’re paying for.

How long does construction take?

It depends on the design, permitting and weather. Before we start, we give you a realistic timeline and keep you updated throughout the build.

Do you handle permits?

Yes. As a state-certified pool contractor, we take care of the permits and inspections required for your project.

Do you offer a warranty?

Yes. We stand behind our work, and the warranty terms for your project are spelled out clearly in your written proposal.

Should I renovate my pool or build a new one?

If the structure is in good shape, a renovation or resurfacing can give you a like-new pool for less. We’ll inspect it during your free estimate and give you an honest recommendation.

Will I have to deal with multiple contractors?

No. One team handles your entire project, from design to final finish, with a single point of contact.
